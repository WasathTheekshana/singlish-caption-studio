# Code of conduct

This project is for people who make videos and the people who help make this tool better. Everyone is welcome, whatever their language, background or experience.

What I ask of everyone here:

- Be kind and patient. Many people in this project are not programmers, and many are working in their second or third language.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and say so when you are unsure what someone meant.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or unwelcome attention, in any language.
- Keep private things private. Never post someone else's personal details, projects or keys.

If someone breaks these rules, I may edit or remove their comments, close issues, or block them from the repository. That decision is mine as the maintainer.

To report a problem with someone's behavior, use GitHub's private report option on the comment, or open a security advisory addressed to the maintainer if there is nothing else you can use.
